Born
first published or standardised.
Weakened
first published attack better than brute force, even if far from practical.
Broken
an attack shown to work: a collision found, a key or plaintext recovered.
Retired
the first ban, withdrawal or deprecation by a standards body (IETF, NIST, IEEE).

Every date quotes its source (Wikipedia or the RFC itself); tap a row to read the quote. A build check refuses any date whose quote is missing from its source or does not state that year.
